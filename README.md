## Requirements

- Different actors can log in/log out. A customer can view products without logging in but cannot purchase products unless they log in.
- Admin can manage (accept or reject) newly registered accounts (Vendors).
- Admin can accept or reject product posts created by vendors before they become visible to customers.
- Admin can enable or disable specific permissions for vendor roles or individual vendors.
- Customer can browse all approved products and search based on category, price, or product name.
- Product post includes: Vendor Name, Title, Description, Price, Category, Images, Available Units, and Number of Viewers.
- Customer can purchase one or more units of a product if it is available in stock.
- Customer can add products to a favorites list.
- Customer can submit ratings and reviews for purchased products.
- Vendor can manage products (create, list, update, delete) (CRUD).
- Vendor can update product stock and pricing.
- Vendor can view sales history and product performance statistics.
- Vendor receives a notification when a customer purchases one of their products.
